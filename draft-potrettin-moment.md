# Potrettin Moment

In a wedding ceremony especially those that are held in the west where many rich people can spend a lot of money they can buy a lot of disposable camera.
the idea is that the guest can take that camera and take a picture of the moment, and the photo can be used as a memento of the moment.
Later on the guest return the disposable camera. And the brides and grooms can have a more rich and authentic moment captured by their guest rather than just
relying on photographers.

so i was thinking how about I create a website that'll do just that!

here's the flow:

1. the guest scan available QR code displayed across the wedding venue, the QR code should also inform that there'll be prize for the most liked photo and photo that are choosen by bride and groom will get special prize later on
2. the QR code will refer to moment.potrettin.com and the guest will see a welcome page for this event
3. the prize will be informed there and also the 2 winning category (most liked photo and photo that are choosen by bride and groom)
4. there's a CTA button to participate in the process. once clicked a UI that resembles photo will be shown. they are required to take a selfie first then submit their name (need validation to make sure they are indeed taking selfie or to put simple just detect whether there's a face in the pic or not)
5. they'll be given a task to submit 5 photos, each time they take picture and they like it it will be uploaded
6. they also can see other guest submission in the UI/UX of instagram story
7. once they reached the limit of 5 photos, they'll get a photo strip generated image that they can download and share with their friends
