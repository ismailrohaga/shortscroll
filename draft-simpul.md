lately i've been planning for my wedding, from attending wedding expo for searching venue and vendors, to calculating the budget and everything.

what i can understand is that there are a lot of components that need to be considered in wedding planning, but the main key aspect is venue and vendors which came with a lot of services such as catering, organizers, entertainment, etc.

and on top of all the most important factors is the religion and culture between the two family because it can determine the location, dates, concept, etc.

i'll summarize what i did for my wedding planning, below are the outlines but im stil at step no 3:

1. searching
   - venue
   - vendors
   - requirements
     - concept (adjusting to my family and her family culture)
     - budget
     - dates
   - accomodation (place to live after the wedding)
2. compiling
   after searching, i've got a list of venues, vendors, and dates, and i've calculated the budget. it's time to compile it all together. i use both microsoft excel and word. i compile about 2 option to decide, and also in this phase i did a projection for personal savings for to help the budgetting
3. deciding
   in this phase i've sent the wedding plan proposal to my parents and her family, there will be some revisions and adjustment to the prepared proposals
4. choosing
   after all is decided its time to contact all of the identified vendors, venues and accomodation to make sure they can provide the services and the dates, doing some dealings and finally payments
5. executing
   after all is decided, i've got the dates, the budget, and the concept, i've got to make sure everything is ready for the wedding day, such as doing some pre wedding photo session, MCU, and some survey to the accomodation that we choose
6. wedding
7. living
   after the wedding, i've got to make sure i can live in the place i've chosen, and also i've got to make sure the accomodation is ready and can be rented for minimal 1 year

all of the process above is done by myself, and it kinda bit cumbersone since i have work to do. i've found some platform such as bridestory where i can search all the vendors and venues, though it's helpful but it's just frustating to use and i ended up searching the vendor in wedding expo or social media.

not to mention to search accomodation, most platform i found are meant for renting for short time, such as for vacation or staycation

all in all, the process of compiling is just not practical, i need to go back and forth between my browser and the app.

and with all of my experience, i have an idea to build a platform that can help me to search and compile the wedding planning, and also help me to make sure everything is ready for the wedding day for up to searching the accomodation options.

in bridestory i can only search vendors and venues, with a bonus of having access to digital wedding invitation
in airbnb/jendela360/travelio/etc i can search accomodation but with limited set of list and filters
in word and excel i can compile the wedding planning and also do some budgeting

imagine if i can search all the vendors and venues, and also the accomodation, and compile the wedding planning, and also do some budgeting and planning for the wedding day all in one platform, it would be a lot easier and more convenient.
